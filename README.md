# The line

> After thirty years of mopping the same school floors, the janitor watched his daughter cross the graduation stage, and when they called her name he stood up first — the only one in the room who knew what that walk had cost.

One line, 223 characters: a quiet, earned moment of pride. Thirty years
of unseen work — mopping the same school floors — behind one walk
across a stage, and the only person standing first is the one who
knows what that walk cost. The pride is earned inside the line, by the
effort the reader can feel, never asserted or explained, and it lands
on the last phrase.
